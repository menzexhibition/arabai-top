Privacy Policy

ARABAI is a free guide website. This policy explains the current static website and planned features that require separate launch rules.

Current Site

ARABAI currently publishes free articles, examples, and links to external AI websites and guides. The site does not currently process payments or run AI tasks for users.

Local Browser Storage

ARABAI may store a small local browser value to control how often the ARABAI Credits note appears. This is used to keep the site calm and avoid showing the same note too often.

External Links

When you open an external AI tool, that website has its own privacy policy. Check the official website before uploading private files, customer data, passwords, or payment details.

Update List

ARABAI may later add an update list, waitlist, or feedback form. Before collecting emails, WhatsApp numbers, Telegram handles, or messages, the form provider and data use should be clearly shown.

Future Digital Access

If ARABAI launches paid digital AI access, payment, account, usage, refund, and data handling rules must be added before accepting payments.

Contact

For support, policy questions, or payment-related issues, contact support@arabai.top.
