Refund Policy

This page explains how ARABAI will handle refunds if future digital products, digital AI access, or paid access are launched.

Digital products and access

ARABAI does not currently accept payments. If future digital products or digital AI access are launched, delivered digital access, downloadable files, or other digital access may be non-refundable after successful delivery.

Pricing must be visible before checkout

Before any paid launch, the product name, what the buyer receives, whether it is a one-time payment or subscription, and the exact price must be shown clearly on the sales page or checkout page.

No refunds after delivery

No Refunds apply after successful delivery of digital access, unlocked access, downloadable packs, or any completed digital fulfillment.

Duplicate charges or technical errors

If you are charged more than once for the same order, or if ARABAI clearly fails to deliver the purchased digital access or digital item, contact support for review and correction.

Future subscriptions

If ARABAI later opens subscriptions, the subscription terms, renewal timing, cancellation rules, and whether any partial refund is possible will be stated on the checkout page before purchase.

Before paid launch

If a product is still marked coming soon, no real payment should be collected for it until checkout, delivery flow, and support contact details are fully live.

Contact

For billing questions, duplicate charges, or delivery issues, contact support@arabai.top.
