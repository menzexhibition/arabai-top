## Shoot 1: Prompt -> Organized Prompt -> Small Sample

### Main pages this supports
- `what-is-a-prompt`
- `organize-prompt-first`
- partly supports `write-with-ai`
- partly supports `make-a-plan`

### Why record this first
- This is the missing bridge between “I have an idea” and “I can really use AI.”
- One good recording can support multiple articles.

### Tool
- ChatGPT preferred
- Gemini or Claude can be backup

### Story
A normal user has a rough business idea but does not know how to ask AI correctly.

### Final lesson
You do not need a perfect prompt on day one.
First speak normally.
Then ask AI to:
1. repeat what it understood
2. ask what is missing
3. turn everything into one clean prompt

### What to show on screen
1. Open a clean new chat
2. Paste a messy human-style request
3. Ask AI not to do the final task yet
4. Let AI repeat its understanding
5. Let AI ask missing questions
6. Answer those missing questions briefly
7. Ask AI to create one final copy-ready prompt
8. Ask AI for one tiny sample result
9. Show the final cleaned prompt clearly

### Suggested messy input
```text
I have a new weekend offer idea for my small coffee shop in Riyadh, but my thoughts are messy.
I want something simple that can bring more people on Friday and Saturday.
Maybe a saffron iced latte or a family offer.
I need something for Instagram, maybe a small plan, maybe a few customer messages too.
I do not know what is the best way to ask AI for this.
Do not create the final content yet.
First tell me what you understood.
Then ask me what information is missing.
After that, turn my messy notes into one clean prompt I can use.
```

### Short follow-up answers to give AI
```text
Target customers: families, students, nearby office workers.
Tone: friendly and simple.
Language: English.
Goal: more weekend visits.
Offer should be realistic for a small shop.
```

### Final prompt you want AI to generate
The exact wording can vary, but the final prompt should clearly include:
- business type
- city
- target customers
- goal
- tone
- output needed

### Final sample result to request
```text
Now use that final prompt and show me only one small sample:
write one Instagram caption and one short customer message.
```

### What screenshots to save
- Messy original request
- AI understanding summary
- AI missing questions
- Final cleaned prompt
- Small sample result

### What article text can say afterward
- “This is the moment where your rough idea becomes AI-ready.”
- “Do not jump to the full task before AI proves it understood you.”

## Shoot 2: Real Writing Task + Rewrite Round

### Main pages this supports
- `write-with-ai`
- partly supports `chatgpt-advanced`
- partly supports `doubao-advanced`

### Why record this second
- Writing is the easiest and most believable first success for beginners.

### Tool
- ChatGPT preferred
- Doubao backup if available

### Story
A small shop owner needs to tell a customer that an order is delayed by two days, but does not want to sound rude.

### Final lesson
AI should not only write.
It should rewrite until the message sounds human, calm, and ready to send.

### What to show on screen
1. Open a clean new chat
2. Paste the rough customer message
3. Ask AI to rewrite it for WhatsApp
4. Read the first result
5. Ask AI to make it shorter and warmer
6. Ask for one more version if needed
7. Show the final chosen version

### Suggested raw input
```text
Rewrite this customer message for WhatsApp.
Make it clear, polite, warm, and not too long.

Original:
Hi, your order is late. It will come after two days. Sorry.
```

### Suggested refinement request
```text
Make it a little warmer and more natural.
Keep it short.
Do not sound too formal.
```

### Final result you want
A clean customer-ready message such as:
- apology
- simple reason or timing
- calm tone
- no robotic language

### Bonus step
Ask:
```text
Give me 3 versions:
1. very short
2. warm and friendly
3. slightly more professional
```

### What screenshots to save
- Original rough message
- First AI rewrite
- Refinement request
- Final chosen version
- 3-style comparison if available

### What article text can say afterward
- “The first answer is only draft one.”
- “A normal user should learn to ask for tone changes, not just accept the first result.”

## Shoot 3: Messy Notes -> Table -> Business Summary

### Main pages this supports
- `spreadsheets`
- partly supports `summarize-documents`
- partly supports `learn-something`

### Why record this third
- This proves AI can help with practical office work, not only chatting.

### Tool
- ChatGPT preferred
- Doubao / Gemini backup

### Story
A perfume shop owner has several days of sales written as plain text and wants a clean table plus quick business answers.

### Final lesson
AI can turn messy notes into a table and explain what matters.

### What to show on screen
1. Open a clean new chat
2. Paste messy sales notes
3. Ask AI to build a clean table
4. Ask for total sales
5. Ask for average daily sales
6. Ask for best-selling product
7. Ask AI to show formulas for Excel
8. Show final result clearly

### Suggested input
```text
Turn these sales notes into a clean table.
Then calculate total sales, average daily sales, and best-selling product.
Use easy English.
Show the formulas I can copy into Excel.

Sales notes:
May 1 Oud Oil quantity 2 sales 600 SAR
May 2 Bakhoor quantity 3 sales 450 SAR
May 3 Oud Oil quantity 3 sales 900 SAR
May 4 Perfume Spray quantity 2 sales 350 SAR
```

### Good follow-up request
```text
Now explain the result like I am a small shop owner, not a spreadsheet expert.
Tell me:
1. total sales
2. average daily sales
3. top product
4. one simple action I should take next
```

### Final result you want
- Clean table
- Clear total
- Clear average
- Best product
- One business takeaway

### What screenshots to save
- Messy notes input
- AI clean table
- Formula section
- Business explanation

### What article text can say afterward
- “You do not need to love spreadsheets to get value from them.”
- “AI can clean the notes first, then help you understand the story inside the numbers.”
